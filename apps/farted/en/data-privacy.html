for the app “Farted”

No data protection officer has been appointed.

2. TYPES OF DATA PROCESSED

The app processes only the following data:

  • The entries you log, including the date and time and the ratings you assign (loudness, duration, stench, relief).
  • An optional location label per entry. If you enable location access, the label is derived on the device and can be edited or removed at any time.
  • Your app settings, such as the selected share card style.

No metadata, device identifiers, telemetry data, or crash logs are collected or transmitted to third parties.

This data is stored exclusively locally on the device. If you enable iCloud sync, it is additionally stored in your own iCloud account.

3. PURPOSES OF PROCESSING

The data is processed for the following purposes:

  • Saving and displaying your entries, statistics, and records within the app.
  • Generating share cards and exports on the device, at your request.
  • Synchronising your entries between your own devices, if you enable iCloud sync.

No use is made for advertising, profiling, or analytics purposes.

4. LEGAL BASIS

Processing is based on Article 6(1)(b) GDPR, insofar as data processing is necessary to provide and use the app’s functions. Location access and iCloud sync are optional and are based on your consent under Article 6(1)(a) GDPR, which you can withdraw at any time in the system settings.

5. RECIPIENTS AND THIRD PARTIES

No personal data is shared with third parties.

In particular:

  • no server or cloud connection by the provider,
  • no analytics services,
  • no tracking services,
  • no advertising services,
  • no profiling,
  • no transfers to third countries.

The app does not use external services to transmit or evaluate the data. Share cards and exports leave the app only when you actively share or save them yourself; the recipient is then determined solely by you.

6. STORAGE PERIOD

The data is stored exclusively locally on the device, and in your own iCloud account if you enable sync.

It remains there until it is deleted within the app or the app is removed from the device.

After uninstallation, locally stored data is generally deleted. Note: Depending on the device settings, local app data may be included in device backups, in particular iCloud backups. The provider has no access to these backups.

7. DATA SUBJECT RIGHTS

Data subjects have the following rights under the GDPR:

  • Right of access,
  • Right to rectification,
  • Right to erasure,
  • Right to restriction of processing,
  • Right to object,
  • Right to data portability.

Since the data is processed exclusively locally on the device, most rights can be exercised directly by editing or deleting entries, exporting them to CSV or JSON, or uninstalling the app.

Requests regarding data protection rights can be sent to info@mannabri.de.

8. DATA SECURITY

The app does not transmit any data to external servers and does not store any data in its own cloud infrastructure. Optional sync runs through your own iCloud account, to which the provider has no access.

No additional encryption of app data is implemented by the provider. The security of locally stored data therefore depends primarily on the security mechanisms of the iOS device used and the system settings selected there.

The app itself has no access to iCloud backups or other backup processes initiated by the operating system.

9. CHANGES TO THIS PRIVACY POLICY

The controller reserves the right to amend this Privacy Policy as necessary so that it always reflects the actual technical and legal circumstances.

The current version is available in the app or at the designated location.
