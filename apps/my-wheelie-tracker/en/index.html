Built for riders who want real data

My Wheelie Tracker helps riders analyze their sessions with clear ride data and motion-based wheelie detection. It is designed for riders who want a simple way to review route history, compare sessions, and study wheelie performance over time. The app is for analysis and recording, not encouragement of unsafe or illegal riding.

Features

  • Live Ride Tracking

    See your route on a live map. Wheelie duration is visible at a glance, so you always know how your session is going.

  • Wheelie Detection

    Track your bike's angle continuously — every wheelie is marked the moment it happens, including duration and position on the map.

  • Your Data

    Keep your data yours — stored locally, never shared, never sold.

  • Session Review

    Get a full session summary after every ride: total duration, number of wheelies, and your best wheelie of the session.

  • Your Progress

    Browse your full ride history and see your best wheelie duration over time.

  • Best Wheelie

    Best wheelie duration tracking.

Safety Notice

My Wheelie Tracker is not a safety device and does not replace rider judgment. Wheelies and other stunts can be dangerous and may be illegal in public traffic or in certain locations. Use the app only where it is lawful and safe to do so, and stop recording immediately if conditions become unsafe.

Data Privacy

My Wheelie Tracker collects GPS location and motion data, processes all data locally on your device, and does not share any personal information with third parties.
