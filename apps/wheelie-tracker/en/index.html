Wheelie Tracker

Track your rides. Detect wheelies. Review every session.

Wheelie Tracker is a GPS ride-tracking app for iPhone that records your route, measures bike pitch using motion sensors, and automatically detects wheelies during a ride.

Built for Riders Who Want Real Data

Wheelie Tracker helps riders analyze their sessions with clear ride data and motion-based wheelie detection. It is designed for riders who want a simple way to review route history, compare sessions, and study wheelie performance over time. The app is for analysis and recording, not encouragement of unsafe or illegal riding.

Features

  • Live Ride Tracking

    Live ride tracking on a map.

  • Wheelie Detection

    Automatic wheelie detection from motion sensor data.

  • Ride Stats

    Ride stats including total distance and duration.

  • Session Details

    Session details with a route overview.

  • Best Wheelie Time

    Best wheelie duration tracking.

  • Local Storage

    Local storage for past recordings on the device.

Safety Notice

Wheelie Tracker is not a safety device and does not replace rider judgment. Wheelies and other stunts can be dangerous and may be illegal in public traffic or in certain locations. Use the app only where it is lawful and safe to do so, and stop recording immediately if conditions become unsafe.

Data Privacy

Wheelie Tracker collects GPS location and motion data, processes all data locally on your device, and does not share any personal information with third parties.
